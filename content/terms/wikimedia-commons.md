A free online repository of openly licensed media files (images, audio, video) maintained by the Wikimedia Foundation. All files must be freely licensed (CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA, or public domain); making them reusable globally by anyone for any purpose. Z

## Details

- Repository underlying Wikipedia’s images
- GLAM institutions upload digitised collection items here to maximise public reach
- Wiki Goes MEK! project (Museum of European Cultures, Berlin) is a prominent example of structured GLAM–Wikimedia collaboration
