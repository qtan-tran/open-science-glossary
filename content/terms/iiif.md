A set of open APIs and community standards that allow images held by different cultural heritage institutions to be shared, compared, annotated, and embedded across websites without centralising ownership.

## Details

- Pronounced “triple-eye-eff”
- Used by the British Library, Vatican Library, Rijksmuseum, Europeana, and 1,000+ institutions globally
- Enables: side-by-side comparison of manuscripts across institutions; deep zoom on high-resolution images; crowdsourced annotation layers
