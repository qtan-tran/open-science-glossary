The practice of making research publications freely available online without price or permission barriers. The Budapest Open Access Initiative (2002) defined OA as free availability on the public internet, permitting users to read, download, copy, distribute, print, search, or link to full text, without financial, legal, or technical barriers.

## Details

- Two primary routes: Green (self-archiving in a repository) and Gold (publishing in an OA journal or platform)
- Gold OA subcategories: Diamond (no fees), Gold (APC-funded), Hybrid (optional OA in a subscription journal)

> [!WARNING]
> OA ≠ open science: OA is one component of open science, which also includes open data, open methods, open peer review, open educational resources, and equity
