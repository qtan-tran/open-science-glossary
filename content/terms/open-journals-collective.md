The Open Journals Collective (OJC) is an international collective of scholars, libraries, university presses, and non-profit academic publishers that builds a sustainable, community-led funding base for independent diamond open-access journals. It is not a publisher itself: it pools library support and collective investment so that journals charging no author-side fees can keep publishing without paywalls, as a systematic alternative to corporate big deals.

## Details

- **Mechanism:** Participating libraries join through a shared membership that funds editorial work, hosting, and preservation across the journals in OJC's collections, rather than paying per article or per journal.
- **Scale:** As of late 2026, OJC's launch collections cover around 260 journals; since January 2026 it has been running a global investment campaign that has raised a multimillion-pound share of its stated target, and its growing library membership base demonstrates collective demand.
- **Open science link:** The model directly supports bibliodiversity: it gives small society and university journals, especially outside major publishing markets, the funding scale no single institution could provide, and takes the pressure off a diamond-oa transition that leaves flagship infrastructure outside the safety net.

> Campaign figures (journals, amounts, members) move quickly; readers should take current numbers from the collective's own pages rather than from this entry.
