The Open Journals Collective is a community-led funding initiative that enables libraries to collectively support independent, non-profit open-access journals through a shared membership model, rather than per-article fees.

## Details

- **Mechanism:** Participating libraries pay into a common pool; journals that charge no author-side fees receive predictable support for editorial work, hosting, and preservation.
- **Why it exists:** Small society and university journals, especially outside major publishing markets, rarely have the scale to fund diamond open access on their own and often vanish when volunteer editors burn out.
- **Open science link:** The model directly supports bibliodiversity and takes the pressure off a diamond-oa transition that leaves flagship infrastructure out of the safety net.

> [!NOTE]
> Because the initiative is young and small, participants should follow its current membership and governance details occasionally on its own channels.
