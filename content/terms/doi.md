A persistent, unique alphanumeric string assigned to a digital object (article, dataset, chapter, preprint, or report). DOIs resolve to the object’s current URL even if the URL changes, making them far more reliable for citation than web addresses.

## Details

- Format: 10.XXXX/XXXX; e.g. 10.1038/nature12373
- Registered by CrossRef (publications) or DataCite (data and other outputs)
- Resolves via https://doi.org/[DOI]
- Zenodo issues free DOIs to any deposited file

> [!WARNING]
> A DOI guarantees persistent identification, not open access; a paywalled article has a DOI but reading it may still require a subscription
