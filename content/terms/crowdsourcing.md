The practice of distributing research tasks to a large group of contributors; often volunteers; via an online platform. In scholarship, crowdsourcing is used for transcribing historical handwriting, classifying images, annotating data, and translating texts. D

## Details

- Major platforms: Zooniverse, Transkribus, Library of Congress “By the People”, WikiSource
- Quality is high when tasks are well-designed and validation is built in
