A nonprofit organisation, founded in 2020 by French research-community leaders, that runs thematic communities of researchers who review preprints for free and publish recommendations of the good ones. A preprint recommended by a PCI community is certified as having passed rigorous peer review, without ever entering a journal: the peer review is public, the reviewers choose whether to sign, and the recommendation carries its own DOI and citation.

## Details

- Splits the two jobs of a journal: certification (PCI does it, in public) and hosting of the version of record (any repository or journal can do it, including its own diamond open access offshoot, Peer Community Journal, launched in 2021)
- Adoption is growing steadily: in 2025 the communities handled 521 submissions and issued 248 recommendations, and Peer Community Journal has published hundreds of articles
- Each community (PCI Zoology, PCI Ecology, PCI Paleontology, PCI Mathematical and Computational Sciences and others) sets its own standards within a shared review platform

> [!WARNING]
> A PCI recommendation certifies a specific version of a preprint: if the authors later publish a substantially revised version in a journal, the two documents are scientifically linked but editorially independent
