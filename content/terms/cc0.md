A legal tool that allows creators to waive all copyright and related rights in their work globally, placing it as close as possible to the public domain. Nothing is owed to the creator; no attribution, no share-alike conditions.

## Details

- Standard licence for datasets uploaded to Wikimedia Commons (enables free reuse without attribution requirements)
- Used by many GLAM institutions for digitised collections
- Distinct from CC BY, which requires attribution to the creator
