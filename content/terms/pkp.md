A research and infrastructure project, founded by John Willinsky in 1998, that develops free open-source software for managing and publishing journals, monographs and preprints, hosted at Simon Fraser University. Its systems, above all Open Journal Systems (OJS), run a substantial portion of the world's non-commercial, fee-free (Diamond) open access publishing.

## Details

- First-wave new entry (D-5), maintainer-reviewed on 2026-11-20
- Main software: Open Journal Systems (OJS), Open Monograph Press (OMP), Open Preprint Systems (OPS)
- As open-source PHP software it is also a commons: community plugins, translations and hosting services grow around it
- Institutional significance: PKP undermines the claim that publishing infrastructure must be rented from commercial providers

> [!WARNING]
> Free software lowers the licence cost, not the total cost: running OJS well still requires editorial labour, server administration and long-term archiving
