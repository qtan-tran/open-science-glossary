Subscribe to Open (S2O) is a collective funding model in which a journal's existing subscribers are asked to continue their payments as "contributions"; if enough of them do, the new volume is published open access for all readers, with no APCs charged to any author. It was pioneered by Annual Reviews from 2020.

## Details

- **Mechanism:** Subscriptions become community support; the publisher sets a target for contributions each year and, when reached, releases the content openly (usually under a CC license).
- **Strengths:** Authors pay nothing, no new money is required, and nonprofit society journals can flip without negotiating transformative agreements publisher by publisher.
- **Risks:** Open access depends on the annual threshold being met; each year is effectively a new crowdfunding round, so budget planners cannot guarantee openness in advance.

> [!NOTE]
> S2O has inspired adaptations beyond journals, e.g. the opening-the-future model for monographs, and is watched as an alternative to both APC gold open access and pure diamond subsidies.
