An organisation (often operating commercially) that produces and sells fake academic papers; with fabricated data, fake or for-hire authorship, or recycled genuine research; allowing purchasers to claim authorship for a fee.

## Details

- Pre-AI: labour-intensive operations concentrated in certain countries and disciplines
- Post-AI: scalable to previously impossible volumes; LLMs can generate plausible abstracts and full papers with realistic-sounding fake citations
- Linked to “paper mill journals”; predatory journals that prioritise revenue over quality control
- Detection: Retraction Watch, PubPeer, image forensics (Proofig), citation verification
