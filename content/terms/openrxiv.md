An independent nonprofit organization established in 2025 to take over the long-term operation of the bioRxiv and medRxiv preprint servers from Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory.

## Details

- **Mission:** To secure the financial and organizational sustainability of the two preprint servers as community scientific infrastructure, rather than leaving them dependent on a single host institution.
- **Operation:** After the transition, submissions to bioRxiv and medRxiv continue working as before; authors and readers should notice no break in service, DOIs, or archives.
- **Funding model:** openRxiv is developing a diversified support base, including institutional memberships, so that preprint infrastructure does not depend on any single funder.
- **Significance:** The move is part of a broader shift toward community-governed scholarly infrastructure, following several years of debate about how to sustain preprint servers after their massive growth.

> [!NOTE]
> As an organization founded in 2025, openRxiv's governance and funding arrangements are still young; consult its own site for current details.
