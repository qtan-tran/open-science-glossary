The Latin American Council of Social Sciences (Consejo Latinoamericano de Ciencias Sociales); a network of 700+ research centres and universities in 52 countries that promotes open access, regional knowledge production, and scholar-led publishing in the social sciences and humanities. CLACSO operates its own institutional repository and publishing platform, supporting Diamond OA in Spanish and Portuguese.
