An open-source journal management and publishing platform developed and maintained by the Public Knowledge Project (PKP) at Simon Fraser University, Canada. First released in 2001.

## Details

- Used by 30,000+ active journals in 130+ countries
- Free to install and use; supports multilingual publishing, ORCID/DOI integration, and open peer review workflows
- Powers many SciELO and Redalyc journals and thousands of university and society journals
- The technical backbone that makes Diamond OA feasible at low cost
- Companion platforms: OMP (Open Monograph Press) for books; OPS (Open Preprint Systems) for preprint servers
