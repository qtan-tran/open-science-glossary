Wikipedia is a free, multilingual online encyclopedia written and maintained by a volunteer community. Launched in 2001 and hosted by the nonprofit Wikimedia Foundation, it exists in hundreds of language editions and is one of the most visited websites in the world. Its content is openly licensed and its editorial model is an emblematic case of crowdsourcing.

## Details

- **Model:** Anyone can edit most articles; quality is maintained through core content policies, discussion pages, and community patrolling, rather than through a central editorial board.
- **Core policies:** Neutral point of view, verifiability (statements must cite published, reliable sources), and no original research: the encyclopedia summarizes existing knowledge, it does not publish new findings.
- **Open science link:** For many readers, and often for researchers too, Wikipedia is the first contact with a topic, and its references form a bridge to the scholarly literature. Partnerships between volunteers and cultural institutions, from smaller edit-a-thons to initiatives like Wiki Goes MEK, feed openly licensed images and sources back into the encyclopedia.
