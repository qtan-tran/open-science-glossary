The final, publisher-formatted, typeset, and copy-edited version of a paper; the authoritative published form. This is what appears on the journal’s website with pagination, DOI, and publisher branding. W

## Details

- Often the version behind a paywall
- Authors typically sign away rights to the VoR in copyright transfer agreements; they can usually only self-archive earlier versions (the Accepted Manuscript)
- Distinct from: Preprint (before peer review), Accepted Manuscript (post-review, pre-typeset)
