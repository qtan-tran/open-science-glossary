SPARC (the Scholarly Publishing and Academic Resources Coalition) is an international advocacy organization, founded in 1998 as an initiative of US research libraries, that promotes open access, open science, and open scholarly infrastructure through policy work, community engagement, and institutional advocacy.

## Details

- **Origin:** SPARC was created out of library frustration with journal-price escalation and the Big Deal, as a coalition voice pushing alternatives to dominant commercial publishers.
- **Today:** It is a broad advocacy network covering open access (e.g. the OA Switchboard-adjacent work), open education, open data, and anti-consolidation campaigns like its Big Deal cancellation tracking.
- **Open science value:** Few organizations have been as consistent in connecting library economics, funder mandates, and researcher incentives into one advocacy program.

> [!NOTE]
> SPARC's regular landscape analyses of open infrastructure are a good first-reference point for anyone trying to map the open ecosystem.
