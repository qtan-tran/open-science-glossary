An open-source platform for managing the peer review and publication of academic books (monographs), developed by the Public Knowledge Project (PKP). The book equivalent of OJS.

## Details

- Manages the full book-length editorial workflow: submission, peer review, editorial decisions, copyediting, production, and publication
- Used by small university presses and learned societies to publish open access monographs without BPCs
