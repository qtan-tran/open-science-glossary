A journal or publisher that charges publication fees (APCs) while providing little or no legitimate peer review, editorial services, or quality control; deceiving authors about the nature of the service.

## Details

- Signs: very fast acceptance, very broad scope, aggressive solicitation emails, missing or fake editorial board, not indexed in major databases
- Tool to check journals: Think, Check, Submit (thinkchecksubmit.org); Directory of Open Access Journals (DOAJ)

> [!WARNING]
> The category is contested; “predatory” is on a spectrum and some journals are merely low-quality rather than deliberately fraudulent
