A policy approach, developed by cOAlition S from 2021, under which funders require their grantees to retain sufficient rights over the accepted manuscript and license it CC BY on acceptance. The author then self-archives that openly licensed version in a repository immediately, without waiting out any publisher embargo, because the licence already exists and the publisher cannot revoke it retroactively.

## Details

- Second-wave new entry (approved 2026-11-20): draft awaiting maintainer review
- The strategy works through grant conditions rather than contract negotiation: accepting the funding contractually binds the author to the retention requirement
- The licensed manuscript is the accepted (post-peer review) version, not the published version of record; the typeset file usually stays behind the paywall
- Legal tests of the approach, including cases where publishers demanded waivers, are documented in the coalition's successive annual reviews

> [!WARNING]
> The RRS only covers authors funded by participating organisations, and some publishers ask grantees to sign waivers; authors must check whether their funder applies the strategy and whether a proposed waiver conflicts with it
