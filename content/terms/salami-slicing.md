The practice of dividing a single research study or dataset into multiple smaller publications (each presenting a fragment as a complete paper); to maximise publication count from one body of work. Also called “LPU (Least Publishable Unit) publishing”.

## Details

- Considered a questionable research practice when it misleads readers about the scope of a study
- Inflates publication counts and distorts bibliometric evaluation

> [!WARNING]
> Distinct from legitimate multi-paper series, where each paper genuinely addresses different research questions
