A publishing model in which neither the author nor the reader pays any fee. Diamond OA journals are funded by academic institutions, scholarly societies, national agencies, or consortia.

## Details

- Also called “Platinum OA” or “scholar-led OA”
- The OA Diamond Journals Study (2021) identified 17,000+ active diamond OA journals
- Examples: most SciELO and Redalyc journals, Érudit (Canada), many institutional journals
- The most equitable OA model for researchers globally; particularly important for scholars in low-income contexts

> [!WARNING]
> Diamond ≠ free of labour costs; running a journal requires editing, typesetting, and hosting, funded by institutions rather than fees
