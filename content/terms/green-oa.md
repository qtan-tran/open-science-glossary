A model in which an author self-archives a version of their article (usually the Accepted Manuscript) in a repository; institutional, subject-specific, or general; without paying an APC. H

## Details

- The most equitable OA route: free to publish, free to read
- Subject to publisher-imposed embargo periods
- Consistently shows a citation advantage in bibliometric studies
- Key tool: SHERPA/RoMEO; a database of publisher self-archiving policies
