A 2024 international declaration calling for an open, community-controlled scholarly communication infrastructure; covering not just publications but all research information (metadata, citations, funding data, institutional affiliation). It argues that the infrastructure underpinning open science should itself be open, non-commercial, and governed by the research community rather than by commercial vendors.
