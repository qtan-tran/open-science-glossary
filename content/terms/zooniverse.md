The world’s largest citizen science and citizen humanities platform, hosted at zooniverse.org. Zooniverse allows researchers to build “projects” that present tasks (classifying images, transcribing text, marking objects) to thousands of online volunteers.

## Details

- Hosts 200+ active projects in biology, astronomy, history, humanities, and climate science
- Developed by the Citizen Science Alliance (University of Oxford and partners)
- Used in cultural anthropology and history for: transcribing historical documents, classifying museum photographs, and annotating ethnographic images
