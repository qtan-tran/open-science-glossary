The diversity of publishers, journals, languages, formats, and geographic origins in the scholarly publishing ecosystem. Borrowed from ecology, the concept argues that a healthy knowledge system depends on variety rather than monoculture.

## Details

- Challenges the homogenising effect of Impact Factor rankings, which concentrate prestige in a few Anglophone journals
- Used to justify supporting SciELO, Redalyc, CLACSO, and other regional OA infrastructures
- Particularly important in cultural anthropology, where multilingual and regional scholarship is central
