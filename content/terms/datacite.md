A non-profit organisation that provides DOI registration services specifically for research data, software, and other scholarly outputs beyond journal articles. DataCite DOIs are used by Zenodo, institutional repositories, and data centres.

## Details

- Maintains metadata schemas for datasets, software, and physical samples
- Distinct from CrossRef, which focuses on publications
