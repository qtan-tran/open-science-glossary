The practice of researchers from high-income institutions submitting to journals whose APCs are covered by their institution’s transformative agreements, while researchers at less well-funded institutions; including most Global South scholars; are left to pay full APCs or seek uncertain waivers. The term highlights how OA funding models can reinforce rather than reduce global inequities.
