A persistent digital identifier for researchers providing a unique 16-digit number (e.g. 0000-0001-2345-6789) that distinguishes one researcher from all others regardless of name changes, ambiguity, or institutional affiliation changes. P

## Details

- Free to register at orcid.org
- Integrates with most major manuscript submission systems, funder portals, and repositories
- Allows researchers to maintain a cumulative record of all publications, grants, and employment across institutions

> [!WARNING]
> ORCID identifies researchers; DOI identifies outputs; ROR identifies institutions
