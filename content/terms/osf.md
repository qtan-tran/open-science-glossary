A web platform, run by the nonprofit Center for Open Science (Charlottesville, USA), on which researchers manage projects: file storage, versioning, preregistrations that mint a frozen, time-stamped record with a DOI, preprint posting, supplementary data, and open collaboration. Launched in 2013, it has become the general-purpose workbench of the open science movement in psychology and neighbouring fields.

## Details

- Second-wave new entry (approved 2026-11-20): draft awaiting maintainer review
- Registrations can be made public immediately or embargoed for up to a fixed period, which lets teams share their plans early without tipping a competitive hypothesis
- Since 2025 the Center for Open Science is developing the OSF into a broader Open Science Ecosystem with support from the US National Science Foundation, adding large-scale collaboration features across institutional boundaries
- A registration is not peer review: it certifies what was planned and when, not whether the plan was any good

> [!WARNING]
> Preregistering on a platform is only as meaningful as the discipline around it: outcome-neutral review by journals (registered reports) is the stronger guard against selective reporting and hidden analytic flexibility
