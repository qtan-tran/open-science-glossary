Consortium Couperin (Consortium unifié des établissements universitaires et de recherche pour l'accès aux publications numériques, "Unified Consortium of Higher Education and Research Organizations for Access to Digital Publications") is the French national consortium of higher-education and research institutions. Founded in 1999 and headquartered in Paris, it unites more than 250 universities, research organizations, and grandes écoles. It negotiates access to scholarly information with publishers and promotes open science as a core part of its mandate.

## Details

- **Negotiations:** Couperin negotiates journal packages and transformative agreements for French institutions and has taken public positions in favor of open-access-friendly deals over pure subscription renewals.
- **Open science policy role:** It participates in national open-science infrastructure and advocacy circles (e.g. open-archive and national publication-support initiatives), not just license negotiations.
- **Why it is cited internationally:** Its approach epitomizes one wing of the consortium movement: the consortium as active open-science policy actor, not just a purchasing club.

> [!NOTE]
> Couperin is a good concrete example when "library consortium" is too abstract: it shows a consortium running negotiation, infrastructure, and policy advocacy in parallel.
