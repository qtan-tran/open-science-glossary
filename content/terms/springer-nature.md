A major academic publishing conglomerate formed in 2015 by the merger of Springer Science+Business Media and Macmillan Publishers’ Nature Publishing Group. Publishes Nature, Scientific American, and thousands of journals and books. V

## Details

- Along with Elsevier and Wiley, one of the “Big Three” commercial publishers accounting for roughly half of all academic articles published globally
- Part of the Holtzbrinck Publishing Group
- Participates in transformative (read-and-publish) agreements with many library consortia
