The world’s largest academic publisher by volume, headquartered in Amsterdam and owned by RELX Group (UK). Publishes approximately 2,500 journals including The Lancet, Cell, and the ScienceDirect platform.

## Details

- Revenue (2022): ~€3.3 billion; profit margin ~35%
- At the centre of the “serials crisis”; sustained journal price increases that have forced library subscription cancellations
- Subject to major “big deal” cancellations by German, Swedish, and other universities
- First publisher to sign a transformative agreement in Germany (Projekt DEAL, 2019)
