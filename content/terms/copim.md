COPIM (Community-led Open Publication Infrastructures for Monographs) was a UKRI-funded project (2019-2023) that built open, community-governed infrastructure and funding models for open-access monograph publishing, where scholarly books do not have to rely on commercial publishers.

## Details

- **Background:** Open access has been far slower for books than for journal articles: monographs sell mainly to libraries, so the APC model transfers badly, and heritage presses lack capital.
- **Outputs:** The project built the Thoth metadata and archiving system for open books, the OAPEN library-hosting integration, an OA Books Toolkit, and curated library-collective pilots for funding university-press books.
- **Governance:** Everything was designed to be community-owned and non-commercial, continuing today under successor initiatives and open-source components.

> [!NOTE]
> Where a "big deal" (BPC) is the repository-level equivalent of journal APCs, COPIM additionally moved the infrastructure itself (metadata, archiving, funding) out of commercial hands.
