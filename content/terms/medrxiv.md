The free preprint server for health sciences, launched in 2019 by Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory, Yale University, and BMJ. It lets researchers share medical and clinical manuscripts before peer review while retaining priority of discovery.

## Details

- **Scope:** Covers all areas of health sciences, from molecular medicine to clinical trials, epidemiology, and public health.
- **Clinical safety screen:** Because preliminary findings can be harmful if misread, every submission receives a basic screen, and each paper carries the reminder that it has not been peer reviewed and must not be used to guide clinical practice.
- **COVID-19:** From early 2020 medRxiv became a central venue for rapid sharing of pandemic research, with unprecedented public and media attention.
- **Acceptance:** Most medical journals allow (and many encourage) prior posting on medRxiv.
- **Governance:** Since 2025 the long-term operation of medRxiv and bioRxiv has been transferred to openRxiv, an independent nonprofit organization established to sustain them.

> [!WARNING]
> Preprints on medRxiv are not peer reviewed, not edited, and must not be used for clinical decision-making; media and policy audiences should check whether findings have passed journal peer review.
