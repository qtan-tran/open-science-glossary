One Nation One Subscription (ONOS) is an Indian government programme launched in 2025 that provides nationwide access to scholarly journals for eligible public higher-education and research institutions, through centrally negotiated subscription agreements with major publishers. It is a national subscription model, not an open-access publishing model.

## Details

- **Mechanism:** The central government negotiates one bundle covering journals from major publishers; researchers at eligible public institutions can read them without each institution paying separately.
- **Scale:** The programme covers thousands of journals across tens of publishers and is one of the largest national licensing schemes in the world by population served.
- **Open science debate:** Critics point out that ONOS spends public money on reading rather than on open publishing, running counter to the global shift driven by funding mandates and transformative agreements.

> [!WARNING]
> Because ONOS is new and its contract details are not fully public, watch official Indian government communications and independent analyses for its current terms and cost.
