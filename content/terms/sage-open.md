SAGE Open is a fully open-access, peer-reviewed multidisciplinary journal published by SAGE Publishing since 2010. It publishes methodologically sound research across the social sciences, humanities, and (until recently) science and medicine, funded by article processing charges.

## Details

- **Megajournal model:** Like PLOS ONE and the BMC-series journals, SAGE Open accepts sound research without a novelty filter, which expands the space researchers can publish in but sometimes makes megajournals, including this one, targets of APC-economy criticism.
- **Publication ethics:** SAGE Open is a regular COPE member journal, but also witnessed prominent editorial problems that other open megajournals faced, in a reminder that megajournal soundness policies still lean heavily on peer-review functioning as intended.

> [!NOTE]
> SAGE Open should not be confused with SAGE's collective funding programme (SAGE Community Open Access), which is a different thing entirely: an institutional membership model for funding OA at SAGE.
