Organised replication studies in which multiple teams simultaneously and independently attempt to replicate the same paper, then aggregate and compare their results. The format combines the scientific value of replication with collaborative rigour.

## Details

- Initiated and coordinated by the Institute for Replication (I4R)
- Particularly common in economics (where datasets are often publicly available) but expanding to other disciplines
- Results reveal not just whether a paper replicates, but why it does or doesn’t
