A Wikimedia project, launched in 2003, that hosts a multilingual library of source texts: historical documents, laws, speeches, literary works and other published material transcribed and proofread by volunteers, either in the original language or in translation. Uploaded source material must be free of copyright or released under a compatible free licence, and every page preserves the scanned original alongside the transcription for verifiability.

## Details

- Second-wave new entry (approved 2026-11-20): draft awaiting maintainer review
- The proofreading workflow pairs page scans with a two-column editor, so every transcribed character can be checked against the original image; this makes Wikisource a model for distributed, verifiable digitisation
- For GLAM institutions it is a standard long-tail outlet: works too marginal for a commercial edition can be made full-text searchable at no cost to the holding institution
- Its subdomain structure separates the multilingual coordination wiki from language-specific collections with their own communities and policies

> [!WARNING]
> Wikisource is a transcription library, not a general text dump: material still in copyright, unpublished manuscripts and non-scanned original sources are out of scope in most language editions
