A spectrum of practices that increase the transparency, accountability, and inclusivity of peer review. Ross-Hellauer (2017) identified five key varieties: No single model is universally “correct”; the best approach depends on discipline, career stage, and publication context

## Details

- Open identities; reviewers are named (not anonymous)
- Open reports; reviewer comments are published alongside the article
- Open participation; anyone may comment, not only invited reviewers
- Open interaction; authors and reviewers can dialogue during review
- Open pre-review manuscripts; the paper is publicly available before formal review begins
