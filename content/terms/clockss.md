CLOCKSS (Controlled LOCKSS) is a community-governed "dark archive" that securely preserves scholarly publications worldwide. It is built on LOCKSS technology and jointly funded by libraries and publishers, so that scholarly content survives even if a publisher stops operating or withdraws it.

## Details

- **Dark archive:** Content is replicated across geographically distributed LOCKSS boxes at major libraries, but access is switched on only after a trigger event, not during normal operation.
- **Trigger events:** Examples include a publisher going out of business, ceasing a journal, or withdrawing content; after a trigger, the preserved content is made freely available to everyone through the CLOCKSS website.
- **Governance:** A board of librarians and publishers governs CLOCKSS jointly, with no single party in control.
- **Coverage:** Tens of thousands of journals and other scholarly resources from major publishers and society presses are archived.

> [!WARNING]
> Because access is only triggered, CLOCKSS is an insurance policy for libraries rather than an open-access source; it complements, but does not replace, regular repositories.
