The practice of publicly registering a study’s hypothesis, methods, and analysis plan before data collection begins, typically on the Open Science Framework (OSF), AsPredicted, or a clinical trials registry. R

## Details

- Creates a timestamped record distinguishing confirmatory from exploratory research
- Reduces HARKing and p-hacking by making intended analyses public in advance
- Distinct from Registered Reports: pre-registration is unilateral (researcher logs their plan); Registered Reports involve a journal committing to publish based on the registered methodology
