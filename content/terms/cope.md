COPE (Committee on Publication Ethics) is an international body providing internationally recognized best-practice guidance for editors and publishers on publication ethics: research integrity, peer review, authorship disputes, plagiarism, conflicts of interest, corrections, and retractions.

## Details

- **What it publishes:** The "Core Practices" plus detailed flowcharts and guidelines for handling concrete cases, from suspected plagiarism to who should be listed as an author.
- **Membership:** Thousands of journals worldwide are COPE members, and adherence is often cited in journal policies as the ethical baseline.
- **Forum:** COPE runs a case-discussion forum where member editors can seek advice on live anonymized cases, which has built a rich informal case law of publishing ethics.

> [!WARNING]
> COPE is advisory, not a regulator: it can expel members, but journals themselves decide whether to follow its guidance, so its flowcharts are a floor, not a guarantee of ethical behavior.
