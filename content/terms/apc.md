A fee paid by an author (or their institution / funder) to a journal so that an article is made immediately and permanently open access on the publisher’s website.

## Details

- Typical range: €500 – €11,000+ depending on publisher and journal prestige
- APCs shift publishing costs from the reader to the author, which can exclude researchers from lower-income institutions or countries
- Not to be confused with BPC (Book Processing Charge), which applies to monographs

> [!WARNING]
> Paying an APC does not guarantee publication; it only funds open access after acceptance
