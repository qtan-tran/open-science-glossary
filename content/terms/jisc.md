Jisc is a UK nonprofit that provides digital infrastructure, licensing, networking, and open research services for universities, colleges, and research institutions. Its best-known role in scholarly communication is negotiating national agreements with publishers on behalf of the UK higher-education sector.

## Details

- **Negotiation arm:** Jisc negotiates journal packages and transformative ("read-and-publish") agreements, so individual institutions do not have to bargain alone with major publishers.
- **Infrastructure:** Beyond licensing, Jisc runs national network and identity services (e.g. eduroam and the UK federation) and supports repositories, open-access services, and research-data infrastructure.
- **Guidance:** Jisc publishes advice on open-access compliance (including monitoring UKRI and Horizon requirements) that most UK institutions follow.

> [!NOTE]
> In effect Jisc acts as the UK's national library consortium and infrastructure agency at the same time, so the boundary with bodies like Couperin or SANLiC is that Jisc is also a services organization.
