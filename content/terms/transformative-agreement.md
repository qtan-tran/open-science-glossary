A contract between a publisher and a library consortium or funder that combines reading access to the publisher’s journal portfolio with open access publishing rights for affiliated authors; in a single fee. Designed to transition library subscription budgets into OA publishing budgets.

## Details

- Key agreements: Germany’s DEAL (Springer Nature, Wiley, Elsevier); Sweden’s Bibsam; Netherlands’ UKB
- Criticism: TAs tend to concentrate OA publishing benefits in well-resourced institutions, reinforcing inequities for researchers without consortium coverage (“APC tourism”)
