A non-commercial open access bibliographic database and publishing platform for Latin American, Caribbean, Spanish and Portuguese-language scientific journals, operated by the Universidad Autónoma del Estado de México. Founded in 2003.

## Details

- Hosts 1,200+ journals; approximately 800,000 full-text articles
- Diamond OA: no APCs, no reader fees
- Pioneered XML-first publishing: articles are produced in machine-readable XML before PDF, enabling better indexing, accessibility, and reuse
