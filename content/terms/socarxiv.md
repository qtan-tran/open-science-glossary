An open repository for preprints in sociology and adjacent social sciences, launched in 2016 by a group of sociologists led from the University of Maryland together with the Center for Open Science, and hosted with the support of the University of Maryland Libraries, where the project now runs under the SocOpen name. It imports the arXiv model to a field whose journal pipelines are slow and whose gatekeeping has historically been strict.

## Details

- Founded out of dissatisfaction with multi-year review lags in sociology journals and with the concentration of publication decisions in a small number of elite journals
- Open to working papers, and its stated equity rationale: scholars at less privileged institutions, and those outside tenure-track jobs, can circulate work immediately instead of waiting for gatekeepers
- Frequently paired in this glossary with CLACSO and bibliodiversity: both are attempts to widen who can publish and be read in the social sciences

> [!WARNING]
> Social-science preprints carry disclosure duties that physics rarely faces: human subjects consent, embargoed journal policies on prior posting, and data protection constraints on the accompanying materials.
