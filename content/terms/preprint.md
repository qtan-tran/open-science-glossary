A version of a research paper shared publicly before it has undergone formal peer review. Preprints are deposited on subject or general repositories and are immediately accessible to anyone.

## Details

- Accelerates dissemination: findings can be read months or years before formal publication
- Major preprint servers: arXiv (physics, CS, maths; since 1991), bioRxiv / medRxiv (biology/medicine), SocArXiv (social sciences), PsyArXiv (psychology), Zenodo (general)
- The preprint is distinct from the Accepted Manuscript (post-review) and Version of Record (final published)

> [!WARNING]
> Preprints are not peer-reviewed; results may change significantly after review. Apply critical scrutiny
