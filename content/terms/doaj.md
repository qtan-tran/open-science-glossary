A community-curated directory of open access journals that applies quality criteria (open peer review transparency, licensing terms, no predatory practices) before listing a journal. Launched in 2003 at Lund University and run since 2013 by the charity Infrastructure Services for Open Access (IS4OA), it indexes well over 20,000 journals and works in practice as a "whitelist": inclusion in DOAJ is read as a signal that a journal is a legitimate OA venue.

## Details

- First-wave new entry (D-5), maintainer-reviewed on 2026-11-20
- Inclusion is free, application-based, and periodically re-evaluated; a major 2015-2017 re-application round removed several thousand journals that did not meet the updated criteria
- Most listed journals charge no article processing charges, making DOAJ the main index of the Diamond OA layer of publishing
- Its v1.0.0 glossary appearances were in the Quick-Reference table only; this entry supplies the fuller context (D-11)

> [!WARNING]
> DOAJ listing is a positive signal, not a guarantee of quality; conversely, a young journal not (yet) listed is not necessarily predatory
