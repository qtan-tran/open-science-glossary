A Creative Commons licence that allows anyone to copy, share, adapt, and build upon a work for any purpose; including commercially; provided appropriate credit is given to the original creator. The most permissive licence that still requires attribution.

## Details

- Required by most major funders (Plan S, Wellcome, NIH) for open access publications
- Variants: CC BY-SA (must share-alike), CC BY-NC (no commercial use), CC BY-ND (no derivatives)
