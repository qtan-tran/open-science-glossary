A publishing format in which a journal peer-reviews the research methodology and rationale before data collection begins and issues an In-Principle Acceptance (IPA); a commitment to publish the paper regardless of results, provided the approved methodology is followed.

## Details

- Eliminates publication bias: null and negative results are published
- Prevents HARKing and reduces p-hacking
- Used by 300+ journals across psychology, medicine, ecology, economics, and other fields
