A non-commercial, open access bibliographic database and publishing platform for scientific journals from Latin America, Spain, Portugal, and South Africa. Founded in Brazil in 1997 (FAPESP and CNPq).

## Details

- Hosts 1,200+ peer-reviewed journals; 800,000+ open access articles
- Diamond OA: no APCs, no reader fees; funded by government agencies
- One of the oldest and largest OA platforms in the world; predating the “open access movement” in the Global North
- Indexed by Web of Science and Scopus
