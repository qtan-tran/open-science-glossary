An international consortium of public research funders and foundations, launched in September 2018 by European research funding organisations, that coordinates Plan S: the requirement that work funded by its members be published in open access venues immediately on publication, without subscription paywalls. It has since grown into the most visible funder alliance for open access policy worldwide.

## Details

- Second-wave new entry (approved 2026-11-20): draft awaiting maintainer review
- Instruments developed or coordinated by the coalition: the Plan S principles and their implementation guidance, the Rights Retention Strategy, criteria for transformative agreements and publishers, and Diamond OA support lines
- Its member organisations make open access a condition of grant funding, shifting the default from author volunteering to funder mandate
- The word "S" in Plan S stands for science, shock, speed and shift ("accelerating the transition to full and immediate open access")

> [!WARNING]
> cOAlition S is the funder coalition, Plan S is the policy: the two names are often used interchangeably in casual discussion, but the coalition is the actor and Plan S is the instrument
