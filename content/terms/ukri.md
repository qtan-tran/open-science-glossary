UK Research and Innovation (UKRI) is the United Kingdom's national public research funding organization, created in 2018 by bringing together the seven Research Councils, Innovate UK, and Research England. Its Open Access Policy (effective from 2022) requires immediate open access for peer-reviewed research articles arising from UKRI-funded research.

## Details

- **Policy core:** In-scope articles must be made open access immediately on publication (no embargo), with the CC BY license as the default expectation, via either paying APCs or depositing in a repository under the rights-retention route.
- **Books:** From 2024 the policy extends to monographs and book chapters, which is one of the earliest and most watched national mandates on open-access books.
- **History:** The Finch Report (2012) and the RCUK policy were its ancestors; UKRI's rules now form one of the strictest national regimes, alongside European cOAlition S funders.

> [!NOTE]
> UKRI also funds open-access block grants to universities, which is why compliance monitoring (supported by Jisc services) is organized at institutional rather than individual level.
