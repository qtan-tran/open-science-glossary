Wiki Goes MEK was a GLAM edit-a-thon at the Museum Europäischer Kulturen (Museum of European Cultures), one of the Staatliche Museen zu Berlin. The first event ran from 24 to 26 November 2017 on the theme of toys and everyday culture: Wikipedians improved German Wikipedia articles in an underrepresented subject area and photographed historical toys so that articles could be illustrated with openly licensed images. A sequel, Wiki goes MEK! 2.0, followed under the GLAM on Tour series.

## Details

- **Goal:** Everyday culture (Alltagskultur) is underrepresented in German Wikipedia; the event paired the museum's collection expertise with the editing community to close that gap.
- **Format:** An edit-a-thon plus a photo shoot inside the museum; the images taken were released on Wikimedia Commons for unrestricted reuse in any language edition.
- **Why it matters here:** It shows that GLAM cooperation is practical, low-threshold work: one well-run weekend can leave behind measurably better articles and a reusable image collection, and build a longer-term relationship between a museum and the Wikimedia community.

> [!NOTE]
> The project pages documenting organization and outcomes are in German; they illustrate the standard toolkit of a GLAM event: day-to-day program, lists of articles to improve, and photo releases agreed with the institution.
