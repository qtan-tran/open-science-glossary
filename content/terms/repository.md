A digital archive where research outputs (articles, data, code, preprints, reports) are stored, preserved, and made openly accessible. Repositories may be:

## Details

- Institutional; run by a university or research organisation (e.g. UCL Discovery, TU Munich)
- Subject-specific; for a particular discipline (arXiv for physics/CS; SocArXiv for social sciences; AIATSIS for Australian Indigenous materials)
- General-purpose; accepting any file type and discipline (Zenodo, Figshare, OSF)
- Primary infrastructure for Green OA and open data sharing
