OA2020 (Open Access 2020) is an international initiative launched by the Max Planck Digital Library in 2016 to accelerate the transition from subscription publishing to open access by repurposing existing subscription expenditures, through transformative agreements negotiated between institutions and publishers.

## Details

- **Core idea:** Libraries already pay billions in subscriptions; converting those flows into publish-and-read agreements can fund open access without new money.
- **Mechanism:** Institutions and consortia sign an Expression of Interest, signaling readiness to renegotiate contracts so that the payment covers publishing instead of reading.
- **Legacy:** The initiative shaped the wave of national transformative agreements (Offenes Publizieren in Germany, Jisc agreements in the UK, etc.) that made "read-and-publish" a standard contract category.

> [!NOTE]
> OA2020 itself was a coordination and advocacy instrument, not a legal entity; its function has largely been absorbed by national consortia and ESAC infrastructure.
