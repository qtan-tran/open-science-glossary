A framework for Indigenous Data Governance developed by the Global Indigenous Data Alliance (Carroll et al., 2020). CARE stands for:

## Details

- Collective Benefit; data ecosystems should enable Indigenous peoples to derive benefit from their own data
- Authority to Control; Indigenous peoples have the right to govern data about their communities, lands, and cultures
- Responsibility; those working with Indigenous data must share how it is used and strengthen community governance capacity
- Ethics; Indigenous peoples have the right to ethical, ongoing, relational data practices that go beyond one-time institutional review board approval

> [!WARNING]
> CARE complements FAIR; FAIR addresses technical openness; CARE addresses governance, power, and who benefits
