A wrong done to someone specifically in their capacity as a knower. Philosopher Miranda Fricker (2007) identified two forms: In open science: researchers from lower-income countries or marginalised communities face systemic disadvantages; their work is cited less, published less; not because of quality but because of structural inequities in the publishing system. APCs, Anglophone norms, and IF-based evaluation all contribute. F

## Details

- Testimonial injustice; having one’s knowledge unfairly discounted due to prejudice about identity (gender, race, nationality)
- Hermeneutical injustice; lacking the shared concepts to make sense of one’s own experience
