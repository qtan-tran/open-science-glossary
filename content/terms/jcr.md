The Journal Citation Reports (JCR), published annually by Clarivate on the basis of the Web of Science citation index, is the reference resource for journal-level citation metrics, including the Journal Impact Factor (JIF), used to evaluate and compare scholarly journals.

## Details

- **What they provide:** For each indexed journal: citation counts, the Journal Impact Factor and its variants (5-year JIF, Immediacy Index), and subject-category rankings and quartiles.
- **History:** The reports descend from Eugene Garfield's citation indexing work and are the direct institutional home of the Impact Factor as a commercial product.
- **Open science concerns:** Like the Impact Factor itself, JCR rankings are proprietary, journal-level (not article-level), and skew toward English-language STM journals, making their use in individual assessment a focus of DORA-style reform campaigns.

> [!WARNING]
> JCR data requires a subscription, so its numbers cannot be independently audited; any open bibliometric work must rely on open alternatives (e.g. OpenCitations, OpenAlex-derived metrics) instead.
