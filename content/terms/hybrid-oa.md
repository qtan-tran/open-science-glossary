A model in which a subscription journal offers authors the option to pay an APC to make their individual article immediately open access, while the journal overall remains paywalled for non-OA articles. I

## Details

- Criticised as “double-dipping”: institutions pay both subscription fees and APCs for the same journal
- Plan S permits hybrid journals only if part of a Transformative Agreement with a clear path to full OA
