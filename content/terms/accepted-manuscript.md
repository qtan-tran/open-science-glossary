The author’s final peer-reviewed version of a paper; incorporating all reviewer-requested revisions; but before the publisher has typeset or formatted it. Also called “Author Accepted Manuscript” (AAM). B

## Details

- Usually the version authors may legally self-archive under Green OA
- Often subject to an embargo period before public deposit is permitted
- Distinct from the Version of Record (VoR), which is the publisher’s final formatted copy
