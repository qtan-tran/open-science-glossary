The free preprint server for biology, operated since 2013 by Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory. Posting a preprint on bioRxiv establishes time-stamped priority for new results while manuscripts are being prepared for, or submitted to, peer-reviewed journals.

## Details

- **Scope:** Covers the life sciences, including genomics, neuroscience, ecology, and evolutionary biology. Sister servers for health sciences (medRxiv) and chemistry (chemRxiv, hosted elsewhere) followed later.
- **Moderation:** Unlike arXiv, submissions receive basic screening for spam, offensiveness, and nonscientific content, but no peer review happens before posting.
- **Mainstream acceptance:** Most life-science journals accept submissions of manuscripts already posted as bioRxiv preprints, and many explicitly encourage it.
- **Growth:** Posting rates accelerated sharply after 2020, and the server was central to the rapid public sharing of COVID-19 research.
- **Governance:** Since 2025 the long-term operation of bioRxiv and medRxiv has been transferred to openRxiv, an independent nonprofit organization established to sustain them.

> [!WARNING]
> A bioRxiv preprint is not peer reviewed and should not be reported as established evidence or used to guide practice without expert assessment.
