A long-lasting reference to a digital resource; a publication, dataset, researcher, institution, grant, or instrument; that remains stable even if the resource moves to a different URL. PIDs are foundational infrastructure for open science, enabling reliable citation, discoverability, and machine-readable connections between research outputs.

## Details

- Key PIDs in research:
- DOI; for publications and datasets (CrossRef / DataCite)
- ORCID; for researchers
- ROR; for research organisations
- ISBN / ISSN; for books and journals
- RRID; for research resources (antibodies, cell lines, organisms, software)
