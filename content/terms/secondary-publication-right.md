A legal right allowing authors to make their published articles freely available in a repository even if they signed a copyright transfer agreement with a publisher, after an embargo period.

## Details

- Encoded in German law (§38 UrhG): researchers at publicly funded institutions may deposit their accepted manuscripts after 12 months, regardless of publisher contracts
- Similar rights exist in Denmark and the Netherlands, and are being developed across Europe
- A key Green OA enabler for researchers without APC or transformative agreement coverage
