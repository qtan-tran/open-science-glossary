Research data that anyone can freely access, use, modify and share for any purpose, subject at most to requirements to attribute the source or share adaptations under the same terms. Openness is a property of access and licensing (the "A" in FAIR); it complements but does not replace the findability, interoperability and reusability demanded by the FAIR principles.

## Details

- In practice: data deposited in a repository under an open licence (often CC0 or CC BY), with sufficient documentation and preferably a persistent identifier such as a DOI
- The licence matters: "available on request" or a deposit without a licence is not open data
- Personal, sensitive or Indigenous data can be FAIR without being fully open; CARE and data-sovereignty constraints legitimately limit openness

> [!WARNING]
> Open data and FAIR data are different claims: a dataset can be open but unusable (poor metadata), or FAIR but closed; the strongest reuse cases need both
