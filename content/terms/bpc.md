The monograph equivalent of an APC; a fee paid by an author or institution to make an academic book freely available as an open access e-book. C

## Details

- Typical range: €5,000 – €20,000 per monograph
- A significant barrier to open access in the humanities and social sciences, where the book is the primary unit of scholarly communication
- Several funders (UKRI, Wellcome, DFG) now require open access for funded monographs
