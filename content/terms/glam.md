Galleries, Libraries, Archives, and Museums; the collective term for cultural heritage and memory institutions. GLAM institutions hold the primary sources, objects, and records on which much humanities and cultural anthropology research depends.

## Details

- Increasing role in open science: major digitisation projects (Europeana, Rijksstudio, Smithsonian Open Access) make millions of objects freely available
- GLAM–Wikimedia partnerships (e.g. Wiki Goes MEK) upload CC0 images to Wikimedia Commons for community enrichment and contextualisation
