openCost is a DFG-funded infrastructure project run jointly by Bielefeld University Library, the German Electron Synchrotron DESY in Hamburg, and Regensburg University Library. It develops open standards and infrastructure for recording, exchanging, and analysing all scholarly publication costs: article processing charges, transformative agreement fees, and institutional publishing expenditures.

## Details

- **Motivation:** Libraries and funders are legally required to report publication costs, but this data is locked in custom contract texts and unreadable spreadsheet exports.
- **Approach:** An open data model and API so every institution can publish its cost data in a comparable, machine-readable format, and negotiated agreements can be reconstructed without reading contracts.
- **Open science value:** The OpenAPC dataset is the visible flagship of this push, while openCost supplies the deeper plumbing that all cost transparency infrastructures can share.

> [!NOTE]
> openCost goes beyond APCs: it also covers read-and-publish fees, in which open and subscription charges are deliberately mixed inside one big-deal contract line.
