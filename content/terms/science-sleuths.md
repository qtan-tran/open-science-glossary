Loosely organised communities of researchers, statisticians, and image analysts who identify errors, data manipulation, or fraud in published papers and report them publicly (via PubPeer, Retraction Watch) or to journals and institutions.

## Details

- Tools: image forensics (detecting duplicated Western blots, manipulated figures); statistical anomaly detection; citation verification
- Notable individuals/groups: Elisabeth Bik (image forensics), Nick Brown (“Statcheck”), DataColada team
- Emerged because institutional peer review was too slow to correct problems post-publication
