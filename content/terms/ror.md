An open, community-led registry of unique identifiers for research organisations (universities, hospitals, funders, companies), enabling disambiguation and linking of institutional affiliations across global scholarly databases. S

## Details

- Free to use; ror.org
- The institutional equivalent of ORCID for researchers and DOI for outputs
- Used to disambiguate institutions with different names, abbreviations, mergers, or campus systems
