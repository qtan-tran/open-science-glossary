A time delay; typically 6, 12, or 24 months; imposed by a publisher between the publication date and the date when an author may legally deposit their Accepted Manuscript in a repository under Green OA.

## Details

- Protects publishers’ subscription revenues during the period of exclusive access
- Funder mandates increasingly shorten or eliminate embargoes: Plan S requires immediate open access with no embargo
- The Secondary Publication Right (§38 UrhG in Germany) allows deposit after 12 months regardless of publisher contracts
