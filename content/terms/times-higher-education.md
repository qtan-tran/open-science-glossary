The Times Higher Education (THE) World University Rankings are an influential annual global ranking of universities, assessing institutions on teaching, research environment, research quality (citation impact), international outlook, and industry engagement.

## Details

- **Citation component:** Historically, a substantial share of the THE score came from a citation-impact measure derived from bibliometric data, which has attracted criticism for making universities' standings partly hostage to publication metrics.
- **Influence:** Governments and universities use THE (and its rivals) in strategy, funding, and hiring decisions, creating visibility and governance pressures that flow back into publishing behavior.
- **Reform pressure:** Like the Journal Impact Factor at journal level, university rankings are criticized by DORA and CoARA-type initiatives as a category error: measuring institutions through proxies that say little about research quality.

> [!WARNING]
> Rankings condense complex institutions into one league table with methodology changes year to year, so a rise or fall may reflect indicator redesign as much as real change.
