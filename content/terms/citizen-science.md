Research that involves members of the general public in data collection, classification, analysis, or interpretation; typically in collaboration with professional researchers.

## Details

- Examples: eBird (bird observation), Zooniverse (image classification), Transcribe Bentham (handwriting transcription), Old Weather (ship logs)
- “Citizen Humanities” is the term preferred when the domain is cultural heritage, history, or language
- Distinct from co-production: in citizen science, the public contributes to pre-defined tasks; in co-production, they help define the research questions
