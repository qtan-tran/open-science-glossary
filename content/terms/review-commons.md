Review Commons is a platform run by EMBO and ASAPbio that provides journal-independent peer review: a manuscript posted as a preprint is reviewed once, and the resulting reports are published alongside the preprint, after which the authors can submit to any of a group of referring journals.

## Details

- **How it works:** Authors upload a preprint, referees are recruited, and the refereed preprint plus the reviews is posted publicly; participating journals then accept the manuscript together with the reviews, without repeating the process.
- **Why journal-independent:** Decouples editorial judgment of the work from a single journal, weakening the prestige economy that concentrates review around a few venues.
- **Referring journals:** A broad consortium of journals across the life sciences (including many high-profile ones) committed to accept Review Commons reviews.
- **Openness:** Reports and author responses are posted openly, so review becomes transparent, citable evidence rather than hidden correspondence.

> [!NOTE]
> Review Commons is one of several models, together with Peer Community In, that aim to publish the reviews themselves as open scholarship.
