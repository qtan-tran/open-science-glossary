Reproducibility: obtaining the same results when re-running the original analysis using the original data and code; a minimum standard of transparency

## Details

- Two related but distinct concepts in research integrity:

> [!WARNING]
> The terms are often used interchangeably but the distinction matters: a result can be reproducible (same code = same numbers) but not replicable (the finding doesn’t hold in new samples)
