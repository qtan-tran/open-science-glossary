Opening the Future is a collective funding model for open-access monographs developed by Central European University Press: libraries buy a membership to the press's closed backlist, and the proceeds are used to make new front-list books open access on publication (now also operated with Amsterdam University Press).

## Details

- **Mechanism:** Libraries collectively subscribe to a package of older (still paywalled) books; their fees fund the open publication of each new title, which is then free for all readers.
- **Why this model:** No author or funder pays anything, and small university presses get predictable community support instead of self-defeating book sales.
- **Open science link:** The model is one of the most practical demonstrations of how library demand-purchase budgets can be repurposed, rather than eliminated, on the road to open access.

> [!NOTE]
> Opening the Future uses the logic of Subscribe to Open for journals, adapted for books, and is one of the models COPIM studied and supported.
