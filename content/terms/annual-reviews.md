Annual Reviews is a US nonprofit scholarly publisher, founded in 1932, best known for its "Annual Review of ..." journal series, which publish invited review articles synthesizing research across many disciplines. In the open-access context, it designed and now leads the Subscribe to Open (S2O) model for its journals.

## Details

- **What it publishes:** Highly cited review journals across the life, physical, and social sciences, which researchers treat as entry points to fields.
- **S2O pioneer:** From 2020 Annual Reviews offered existing subscribers a straightforward deal: continue paying (roughly unchanged) and the new year's volumes become open access for everyone if enough subscribers stay.
- **Significance:** Because Annual Reviews did this from a standing subscription base with no APCs, it became the reference case for flipping nonprofit subscription journals to open.

> [!NOTE]
> The S2O logic works only while enough institutions keep contributing; prudence demands planning for what happens if support sags, a risk S2O publishers openly discuss.
