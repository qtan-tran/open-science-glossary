A publishing model in which an article is made immediately and freely available on the publisher’s own website upon publication, typically funded by an APC paid by the author or their institution.

## Details

- Provides immediate, permanent, licensed open access with a full Version of Record
- Subcategories: pure gold (OA-only journals), hybrid gold (OA option in a subscription journal), mega-journal gold (e.g. PLOS ONE)
- Dominant model promoted by funder mandates, but criticised for excluding authors who cannot pay APCs
