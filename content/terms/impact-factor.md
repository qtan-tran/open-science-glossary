A metric calculated annually by Clarivate Analytics as the average number of citations received by articles published in a journal in the two preceding years.

## Details

- Originally designed to help librarians decide which journals to subscribe to
- Now widely misused as a proxy for the quality of individual articles, authors, and institutions
- Criticised for: statistical unsoundness; gaming via review articles; excluding non-English and non-STEM journals; creating perverse incentives
- DORA (2012), CoARA (2022), and the Barcelona Declaration (2024) all call for reducing reliance on IF
