An online database summarising publisher policies on self-archiving; for each journal, it shows which version of an article (preprint, accepted manuscript, or version of record) may be deposited in a repository, under what conditions, and after what embargo period.

## Details

- Free at sherpa.ac.uk/romeo
- Essential tool for Green OA compliance: always check SHERPA/RoMEO before depositing an article
