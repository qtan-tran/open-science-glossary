The Open Access Scholarly Publishing Association (OASPA) is an international membership association founded in 2008 that promotes best practices, standards, and innovation in open-access publishing, bringing together publishers, infrastructure providers, and related organizations.

## Details

- **Membership criteria:** To join, publishers must demonstrate genuine open-access publishing, transparent pricing, and proper editorial governance, which makes OASPA membership a weak quality signal and a filter against predatory outlets.
- **Activities:** OASPA publishes guidance on publication ethics, licensing, and transparency of APCs, and hosts the conference where much of the open-publishing community sets its agenda.
- **Enforcement:** OASPA has suspended or expelled members for publication-ethics breaches, so its actions are one feedback loop the scholarly community watches.

> [!NOTE]
> OASPA membership covers both fully open publishers and open-access operations of commercial houses, so it is an industry-standards body rather than an advocacy group for non-commercial publishing alone.
