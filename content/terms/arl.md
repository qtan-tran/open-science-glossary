The Association of Research Libraries (ARL) is a membership organization of major research libraries in the United States and Canada (university libraries plus national and public research libraries). It shapes policy on scholarly communication, research data, and library economics through statistics, analysis, and advocacy.

## Details

- **Role in open access:** ARL's statements and analyses have been central to library resistance to Big Deal pricing, and SPARC was founded as an ARL initiative.
- **Analytics:** ARL's annual library statistics and its work on scholarly-communication metrics are reference points for library budget discussions worldwide.
- **Open science value:** Its members hold the budgets that fund transformative agreements and open infrastructure collectives, so ARL's positions significantly shape what is economically feasible.

> [!NOTE]
> ARL is North American; European counterparts include Jisc (UK) and national consortia like Couperin (France).
