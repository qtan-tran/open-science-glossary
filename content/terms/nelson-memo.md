The Nelson Memo is the August 2022 memorandum from the US Office of Science and Technology Policy (OSTP), signed by acting director Alondra Nelson, directing federal funding agencies to ensure that publications resulting from federally funded research are made publicly accessible immediately upon publication, eliminating the previous option of a 12-month embargo.

## Details

- **What changed:** Before 2022, US agency rules allowed up to a 12-month embargo before funded papers had to be openly available; the memo requires immediate access and, at agency discretion, encourages deposit in repositories.
- **Scale:** It applies across all US federal science agencies, making the United States federal layer one of the largest single open-access mandates in the world, analogous in spirit to European funder plans.
- **Rights:** The memo pushes authors towards licenses that permit reuse, and raises the same author-rights questions long discussed under Plan S-style rights retention approaches.

> [!NOTE]
> Because implementation details are set agency by agency, the on-the-ground rules (deposit locations, license options) vary; the memo sets the destination, not the exact vehicle.
