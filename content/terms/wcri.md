The World Conference on Research Integrity (WCRI) is the leading international conference series on research integrity, convening researchers, institutions, funders, publishers, and policymakers to address responsible research practices and scientific integrity. The series began in Lisbon in 2007 and has met roughly every two years, with the 9th WCRI held in Vancouver, Canada, in May 2026.

## Details

- **Output statements:** Several conferences adopted consensus statements that anchor the field's policy vocabulary, including the Singapore Statement (2010), the Montreal Statement on research integrity in cross-border collaborations (2013), and the Amsterdam Agenda (2017).
- **Scope:** From individual misconduct (fabrication, falsification, plagiarism) to systemic questions: incentive structures, publication pressure, peer review integrity, and research assessment reform.
- **Open science link:** Recent editions increasingly discuss how open practices (preregistration, data sharing, open peer review) function as structural safeguards of integrity, not add-ons.

> [!NOTE]
> Because it convenes all stakeholder types at once, WCRI is one of the few venues where the integrity and open-science communities update each other's agendas directly.
