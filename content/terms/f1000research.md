F1000Research is an open research publishing platform (founded 2013, now part of Taylor & Francis Group) where articles are published rapidly after internal checks, and then undergo transparent post-publication peer review, with the reviewer reports, author responses, and approval statuses published openly alongside each article.

## Details

- **Model:** Publication first, review after: articles appear online, receive DOIs, and count as published, while invited open peer review determines whether they are "Approved" or flagged with concerns.
- **Transparency of reviewing:** Reviewer identities and reports are public, and articles without any approvals remain explicitly flagged, in contrast to journals whose weak peer review is invisible after acceptance.
- **Open data:** F1000Research mandates data-availability statements and encourages deposit, and is one of the pioneering venues of open-data editorship.
- **Model for funder platforms:** It powers funder-run publishing platforms like Wellcome Open Research and the EU's Open Research Europe, so research-funder workflows can run on this model under funder (not publisher) branding.

> [!WARNING]
> Because "published" precedes "approved", downstream databases miss the nuance; a strict reader must inspect the open status of peer review before citing an article as fully approved.
