A non-profit DOI registration agency operated by the Publishers International Linking Association (PILA). CrossRef mints DOIs for journal articles, books, and conference papers on behalf of academic publishers, and maintains the metadata infrastructure linking citations across publishers.

## Details

- Distinct from DataCite, which mints DOIs for datasets and other research outputs
