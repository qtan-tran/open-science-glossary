---
title: "Open Science Glossary"
description: "A machine-readable, citable glossary of open science terms for students and researchers."
---

A glossary of key terms in open science: publishing models and economics, policy and mandates, research assessment, research integrity, infrastructure, research data, peer review, public engagement, cultural heritage, epistemic justice, and licensing.

Definitions are grounded in the UNESCO Recommendation on Open Science (2021), Fecher & Friesike (2014), and the bibliometric and publishing literature. Entries flag common confusions and cross-reference related terms; the whole content set is available as plain data in the repository.
